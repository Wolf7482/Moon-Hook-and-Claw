Moon, Hook, & Claw™

Welcome to Moon, Hook, & Claw™.

Nestled amongst the Enchanted Evergreens, Moon, Hook, & Claw™ is a small independent creative company dedicated to craftsmanship, storytelling, folklore, fantasy, and community.

Inspired by woodland paths, moonlit clearings, old tales, and curious wonders, we create handmade goods, original creations, small copper works, and custom commissions designed to bring a touch of enchantment into everyday life.

Though our roots lie among the mountains of North Carolina, our doors stand open to wanderers from every corner of the world. We believe good stories, honest craftsmanship, and a little imagination can bring people together regardless of where they call home.

Within these woods you'll find handcrafted wares in Mossy Hollow Mercantile, stories and musings in Whispering Curiosities, custom-made creations within the Custom Cubbie, and whatever other wonders may take root beneath the boughs in the years to come.

Whether you arrive seeking a gift, a tale, a custom creation, or simply a place to linger beside the campfire for a while, we are honored to share our corner of the Evergreens with you.

So wander freely, tread lightly, and may your journey be filled with curiosity, wonder, and a little bit of moonlight.
